> **not** derived from anything on this page.
>
> **Corollary — a quiet drift control is not proof of a clean session.** During
> the discarded attempt recorded in §2 of that file, the control did not move
> while the measured arm's IPC fell 1.52 → 1.44. Watch the measured arm's IPC
> and cycles/row too.
>
> **What this page IS still good for:** the method and pinning, the corpus

## 3. Framing attribution (the closed blind spot)

`StreamSubPhase::Encode` timed only the Arrow array build. The arrow-flight
encoder stage — IPC serialization, `DictionaryHandling::Hydrate`'s per-batch
`RecordBatch` rebuild, and re-slicing every batch that exceeds the encoder's
2 MiB target — was in **no** sub-phase at all, which made levers 4 and 5
unfalsifiable. `stream_encode_framing` now covers it.

Measured over the 4,000,000-row corpus at the production `batch_size` of 8192:

| sub-phase | total | per row | share of encode+framing |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| `stream_encode` (Arrow array build) | 5.7316 s | **1,432.9 ns/row** | 82.1% |
| `stream_encode_framing` (IPC framing) | 1.2520 s | **313.0 ns/row** | **17.9%** |

So **roughly one sixth of the per-row encode cost is IPC framing**, and none of it
was attributable before. (At `batch_size` 64 the split moves to 40.6% framing —
the framing cost is per-message, so it scales with batch count; the 8192 figure is
the production-relevant one.)

These are wall times on CONCURRENT pipeline threads: they overlap, and they do not
sum to the `stream` phase. They attribute WHERE time goes inside one process; the
`perf`-based rows/s and cycles/row in §1 remain the throughput claim.

## 4. Instrumentation overhead of the new sub-phase

* **Metrics OFF** — the state every measurement in §1 was taken in:
  `time_encoder_framing` returns the encoder stream **unwrapped**, so there is no
  adapter in the poll path at all. The cost is one `metrics_active()` branch per
  RPC. The baseline above therefore carries **zero** framing-instrumentation
  overhead, structurally, not by estimate.
* **Metrics ON**: two `Instant::now()` per encoder `poll_next`. Bounded by the poll
  count, which is O(FlightData messages) — on this corpus ~10^3 for a 4M-row scan
  against a 1.2520 s framing bucket, i.e. well under 0.1% of the bucket it
  reports. This is a BOUND from the poll count, not a separately measured delta.

## 5. What remains OWED (not claimed)

* The **WS0 absolutes** (240,100 / 312,155 rows/s) — corpus- and machine-bound;
  not reproduced, not reproducible here.
* The **stock-Cassandra head-to-head leg** — no Cassandra on this box.
* The **compressed-corpus shape** — CQLite's write surface is uncompressed-only
  (#1406), so the LZ4 `chunk_length 16384` geometry of the #3026/#3100 corpora
  cannot be reproduced by this generator.
* Absolute `cycles/row` is counted across BOTH SMT siblings of the pinned physical
  core. The ratio and the arm-to-arm delta are unaffected; the absolute is
  comparable only to figures gathered the same way.
