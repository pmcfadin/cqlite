DOES CONTENTION THREATEN THE AC3 VERDICT (pre-registered total basis)?
  threshold 3.0000%   mean 1.7027%   margin 1.2973 pp
  share spread over the 0.1338 pp across a 5.0x load range
  => flipping the verdict needs 10x the entire observed load-induced variation
  Mechanism: the share is a RATIO measured on a pinned core, so contention slows
  numerator and denominator together -- which is why the fitted slope is ~0 and slightly
  NEGATIVE (-0.00090 pp per unit).

  AC2's answer is PARITY, so the question is whether contention could hide a real effect
  in EITHER direction -- not (as the first, wrong version of this file argued) whether it
  could destroy an anti-concentration finding that did not exist.
  cycle share in-binary : slope -0.00436 pp/unit,  idle extrapolation 3.0068%
  stall share in-binary : slope +0.04405 pp/unit,  idle extrapolation 2.8199%
  ratio at the measured means      1.061x

  stall-share rep spread           0.8307 pp (sd 0.4216)
  => the ratio stays near 1.0 on both readings, but the stall-side spread is WIDE, so an
