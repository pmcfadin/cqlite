## Deviations from the plan
- Quota: freeside enforces the tenant quota on pod **limits** (14 GiB memory, 12 CPU), vcluster system pods use about 2.2 GiB and about 1.25 CPU. Cassandra pods were reduced from 3.5 to 3 GiB (heap 1.25 GiB, young 320 MiB), tools pod CPU limits 0.5+0.5, generator Jobs 0.5 CPU / 256 MiB. The load ran at about 1.8-3 k writes/s per generator pod.
- Data scaled below the plan: 8 M `t_basic` rows (as planned), `t_coll` 1 M, `t_udt` 1 M, `t_wide` 4.5 M (one 2.5 M-row partition), `t_static` 1.5 M, `t_tomb` 1 M live rows plus deletions (the plan had 2 M, 4 M, 3 M, 2+1 M). Largest table 1.79 GB per node (plan: >= 1 GB), largest partition 224 MB (plan: > 100 MB).
- The generator journals the deletion and overwrite operations and the scenario deletes, not every insert (the inserts are a deterministic function of id and seed).
- The plan's t_resurrect BIG case: the resurrection tables are BTI only; the BIG damaged copy comes from `t_tomb nb-6-big`.
- C4 method: only 7 real kills were taken (each OOM kill restarts the runner with CrashLoopBackOff, up to 5 minutes), the other over-budget cells were measured at a raised limit (pod resize to 1800 MiB, the quota maximum) with a 1.7 GiB sampler cap, so some "over 1.7 GiB" rows are lower bounds. Resizing back down is refused while the page cache is charged, so the runner is still at 1800 MiB at the end of the run.
- The Phase 5 `diff` uses `--partition <sampled key>`; light tools ran at 128 MiB with a 120,000 KiB sampler cap, `audit --deep` and `verify full` on damaged copies at the raised limit (their RSS exceeds 128 MiB even on 2 MB copies).
- Healthy `diff` pairs reduced to db0 vs db1 at NOW for the 6 core tables, self-diff for `t_static` only, per-cell timeouts 300/120 s, because each non-finishing cell cost minutes.
- Phase 7 teardown: not done, by instruction. The cluster is owned by the requester.
- Tools-pod uses `/work/bin/rtime` (a 60-line C sampler built on the dev box) instead of `/usr/bin/time -v` (absent on the dev box).
- Per-key `find`/`explain` runs of db1/db2 outputs were produced and compared for explain vs live (all 3 nodes) but `find` vs sstabledump for db0 only.
